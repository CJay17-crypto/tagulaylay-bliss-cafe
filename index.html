About Bliss Cafe

Bliss Cafe is a local cafe located in Dologon, Maramag, Bukidnon, near Central Mindanao University.

The cafe aims to provide affordable and satisfying food and beverages for students, residents, and other customers in the area.

We believe that good food and enjoyable moments should be accessible to everyone.
